About Me

Hi, I'm Areti. I've always been fascinated by people, the way they think, what captures their attention, and the stories that inspire them. That curiosity has shaped both my academic journey and the way I approach life. Whether I'm conducting research, exploring a new city, or learning something completely outside my field, I'm driven by a desire to understand how things work and the people behind them. My academic background is in Business Administration and Management at the University of Patras, where I graduated in December 2024. In order to gain experience, I decided to work for a year as a Marketing Assistant at AVIN Oil, part of Motor Oil Hellas Group. Recently, I completed my Master's in Marketing and Sales Management at EAE Business School in Barcelona, where my research focused on consumer behavior, brand authenticity, and the impact of storytelling in advertising. I enjoy combining analytical thinking with creativity, turning research into insights that help brands communicate more meaningfully and authentically. Long before marketing became my passion, storytelling was already a big part of my life. For 12 years, I was a member of a theater group, performing in numerous productions that taught me confidence, collaboration, discipline, and the power of connecting with an audience. During that time, I also spent three years training as a vocalist at a music school, an experience that strengthened my appreciation for performance, expression, and continuous practice. Together, these experiences shaped my creativity and continue to influence the way I think about communication today. Outside of work, I love discovering new places, learning new skills, and asking questions. I enjoy traveling, exploring nature, and finding beauty in both the small details and the bigger picture. I'm equally happy diving into academic research, planning my next adventure, or spending time outdoors observing the world around me. I believe the best ideas emerge where curiosity meets creativity and empathy. Whatever I'm working on, I aim to approach it with an open mind, genuine enthusiasm, and a commitment to continuous learning. Thanks for stopping by; I hope you enjoy exploring my work and getting to know me.
